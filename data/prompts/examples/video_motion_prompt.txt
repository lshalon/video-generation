## Your Task
Write a SHORT prompt (1-2 sentences) describing the MOTION that should happen in this video. The video model already has the starting frame - you only need to describe what MOVES and HOW.

Focus on:
- Camera movement (slow push-in, pull-back, static, etc.)
- Subject movement (head tilt, hand gesture, subtle expression change, etc.)
- Keep it simple and achievable in 5-10 seconds

DO NOT describe:
- The scene setup (already in the image)
- Lighting or background (already established)
- What the person looks like (already in the image)

Example good prompts:
- "Slow push-in as the model subtly tilts her head and gives a soft smile"
- "The model slowly lowers her hand from her neck while maintaining eye contact, gentle head movement"
- "Static shot with subtle breathing movement and a slight head tilt to the left"

Write ONLY the motion prompt, nothing else.
