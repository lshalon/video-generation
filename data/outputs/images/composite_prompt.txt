## Part 1: Composite Instruction

Place a single tiny gold pavé diamond star stud earring on the model's visible left ear, positioned at the center of the earlobe where a standard lobe piercing would be. The earring is a five-pointed star shape in warm yellow/rose gold, entirely pavé-set with approximately 10-12 small round brilliant white diamonds covering the entire front surface. The star has a classic stud post backing and sits completely flush against the earlobe with no dangle or drop. Based on the on-model reference photo (Figure 4), the star is EXTREMELY small — approximately 4-5mm across, which is roughly 1/5 to 1/4 the width of the earlobe. It should appear as a delicate, subtle sparkle on the lobe, not a prominent statement piece. Given the model's head is turned in a three-quarter profile facing right with her left ear fully visible, the star should sit flat against the lobe surface with a very slight angle matching the natural plane of the earlobe. The gold tone should be a warm, soft yellow gold that catches the studio lighting naturally. Ensure the design is identical to the reference photos — a clean five-pointed star shape fully filled with tiny pavé diamonds, no empty gold space in the center, with gold prong settings visible between stones.

## Part 2: DO and DO NOT List

DO:
- Match the size EXACTLY to the on-model reference in Figure 4 — the star should be tiny and delicate, roughly 4-5mm across
- Use warm yellow gold color matching the product photos
- Place the stud at the center of the earlobe at a natural piercing point
- Add subtle light reflection and sparkle on the diamonds consistent with the studio lighting in the scene
- Keep the earring flush and flat against the earlobe surface
- Include a very subtle shadow behind the earring where it meets the skin
- Maintain the five-pointed star shape with full pavé diamond coverage
- Match the existing warm, soft studio lighting on the earring

DO NOT:
- Make the earring too large — this is the single most common error; it should be barely noticeable at first glance
- Change the star shape, diamond count, or setting style from the reference photos
- Add any earring to the right ear (not visible)
- Alter the model's skin, hair, makeup, facial features, or expression
- Change the background, lighting setup, or overall scene
- Add any additional jewelry, ear cuffs, or piercings
- Make the earring appear to float or hover away from the earlobe
- Use silver, white gold, or platinum tones — it must be warm yellow gold
- Add excessive sparkle or lens flare effects that look unnatural
- Distort the ear anatomy or skin texture around the earring placement
